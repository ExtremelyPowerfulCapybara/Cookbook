molasses-glazed roast pork and sweet potatoes

An easy glaze made from molasses, mustard, and fresh rosemary coat both the pork and sweet potatoes in this cozy supper. Sauté greens or steam broccoli to serve alongside. End the meal with warm gingerbread or ginger cookies.

Pork tenderloins, 2 (about 10–12 oz/315–375 g each)
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
Light molasses, ¼ cup (2.5 oz/75 g)
Dijon mustard, ¼ cup (2 oz/60 g)
Fresh rosemary, 1 tablespoon minced
Red pepper flakes, 1 teaspoon
Orange-fleshed sweet potatoes, 1½ lb (750 g), unpeeled, cut into ½-inch (12-mm) slices
Olive oil, 2½ tablespoons, plus more for brushing
Shallot, 1, minced
Low-sodium chicken broth, ½ cup (4 fl oz/125 ml)
