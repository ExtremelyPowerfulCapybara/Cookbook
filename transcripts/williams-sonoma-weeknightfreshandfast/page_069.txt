Separate block:
quick tips

Here’s another recipe that can be easily doubled. Cooking the greens in
the pasta water adds a touch of flavor and saves you from washing
another pot. Avoid using mass-marketed ricotta cheese, as it has an
inferior flavor and texture, you need the real thing for this sauce.
