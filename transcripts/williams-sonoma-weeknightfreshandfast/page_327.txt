• While the shrimp are cooking, add the pasta to the boiling water, stir
well, and cook until al dente, about 11 minutes.

• Drain the pasta, add to the shrimp sauce, and stir to coat the pasta.
Taste and adjust the seasoning and sprinkle with the fennel fronds.
Transfer to a large warmed bowl and serve right away.

quick tips

To save time, I like to pick up roasted bell peppers and cheese packed in
water from the deli on my way home from a busy day. Keep kalamata
olives on hand in the refrigerator to add zest to salads and pastas.
American shrimp are delicious and are a sustainable seafood choice.
