add a simple side

• Wrap beets in foil and roast at 400°F (200°C) until tender, 1–1½
  hours. Peel, cut into wedges, and toss with vinaigrette and fresh herbs.
• Peel and grate carrots and toss with a mustard vinaigrette.
  Garnish with minced fresh parsley or chopped green onions
• Stir-fry blanched broccoli or cauliflower florets with minced
  garlic and red pepper flakes
• Roast whole sweet potatoes (prick them with a fork a few times
  first) in a 400°F (200°C) oven until tender, about 50 minutes, and
  serve with molasses- and allspice-flavored butter
