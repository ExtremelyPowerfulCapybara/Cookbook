Separate block:
choose fruit for dessert

• Brush pitted and halved yellow peaches with melted butter and
  grill over a medium-hot fire until well browned, about 8 minutes;
  sprinkle with crumbled amaretti cookies or gingersnaps

• Cut plums into thin slices and serve with mango, raspberry, or
  other fruit sorbet

• Cut ripe cantaloupe, honeydew, or watermelon into wedges, chill
  thoroughly, and serve with sea salt and chile powder for sprinkling
