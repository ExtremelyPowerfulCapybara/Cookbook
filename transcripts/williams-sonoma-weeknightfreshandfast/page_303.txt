• Add the wine and boil until reduced by about half, stirring up the browned bits on the pan bottom, about 2 minutes. Return the chicken to the pan. Add the broth and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer, turning after 10 minutes, until the chicken is tender, about 20 minutes. Uncover and simmer until the liquid thickens slightly, about 5 minutes. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

• Meanwhile, bring a large pot three-fourths full of salted water to a boil. Add the noodles, stir well, and cook until just tender, about 7 minutes.

• Drain the noodles and transfer to a warmed shallow bowl. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil and toss to coat well. Divide the noodles among 4 warmed plates and spoon the chicken mixture over the top. Sprinkle servings with parsley, if desired, and serve right away.

Quick tips

Chicken thighs stay juicy when reheated; if you are cooking for two, make the entire recipe and save the rest for a meal on another night. Instead of dry white wine, try an off-dry Gewürztraminer for the stew and then, if you like, pour the rest of the wine with the meal.
