braised chicken with peppers, olives,
and capers

Fresh rosemary, spicy pancetta, rich olives, and tart
capers turn a simple stew into a popular weeknight
meal. Use a mixture of red and yellow peppers for
color, and spoon the stew over bulgur wheat or rice
to complete the meal.

Chicken, 1 (about 3½ lb/1.75 kg), cut into 10 pieces
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
Olive oil, 1 tablespoon
Fresh rosemary, 2 tablespoons minced
Large red or yellow bell peppers, 2, sliced
Large onion, 1, halved and then sliced
Pancetta, 1½ oz (45 g), chopped
Red pepper flakes, ½ teaspoon
Diced tomatoes, 1 can (14½ oz/415 g)
Dry white wine, 1 cup (8 fl oz/250 ml)
Pitted kalamata olives, ⅓ cup (2 oz/60 g)
Capers, 2 tablespoons, rinsed

Separate block:
makes 4 servings

• Pat dry the chicken. Sprinkle on both sides with salt and black pepper.
In a heavy large pot over medium-high heat, warm the oil. Add the
rosemary, then the chicken pieces, in batches if necessary. Cook until
brown, about 5 minutes on each side. Transfer to a plate.
