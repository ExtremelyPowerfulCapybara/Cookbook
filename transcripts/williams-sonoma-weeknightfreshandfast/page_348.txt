Fresh foods for quick weeknight meals

When planning fresh and fast dinners, I start with the fresh produce I find at the farmers market and let it inspire the other ingredients—such as meat, poultry, or fish—in the meal. The same rules apply for buying these ingredients as for fruits and vegetables: I purchase the very best I can find. For the most delicious meals, seek out a butcher, fishmonger, or dairy specialist that stocks top-notch ingredients at reasonable prices, and patronize them regularly.

chicken and turkey

I choose organic poultry whenever possible, as I believe it to have the best flavor and be the most wholesome choice. When I’m in a hurry, I buy pieces that are ready to cook: among my favorites are boneless, skinless chicken breast halves; chicken breast tenders; and boneless, skinless chicken thighs. Cut from the breast meat and flattened, chicken and turkey cutlets are a wonderful choice for quick sautés. When choosing ground turkey for burgers or stews, I favor dark meat, or a mixture of white and dark meat, as it lends more flavor and moisture to a dish than does ground breast meat.
