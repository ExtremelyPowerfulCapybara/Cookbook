• Heat a griddle or large frying pan over medium-low heat. Add 2
tortillas and cook until softened slightly, about 30 seconds. Turn the
tortillas over. Spread 1/4 cup (1 oz/30 g) of the cheese over 1/2 of each
tortilla, leaving a border around the edges. Spread 1/4 cup of the
mushroom mixture over the cheese on each. Fold the empty side of the
tortillas over the filling and press lightly. Cook until light golden and
crisp, about 3 minutes on each side. Transfer to warmed plates. Repeat
with the remaining tortillas, cheese, and mushroom mixture, adding more
tortillas to the griddle after folding the quesadillas in half.

• Divide the quesadillas between warmed plates. Serve right away with
the salsa.

quick tips

This dish can easily be doubled, but you will need to cook the quesadillas
in several batches. Keep the first batch warm in a preheated 200°F
(95°C) oven while cooking the remaining batches.
