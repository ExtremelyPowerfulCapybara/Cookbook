quick tips

I make variations on this basic recipe all fall and winter, adding more
broth for a thinner version, less for thicker; including sausages for a
richer soup; and substituting different vegetables and beans depending on
what I find at the market or have on hand. Leftover pasta, meat, or
chicken adds great flavor. The tastes deepen each time this soup is
reheated.
