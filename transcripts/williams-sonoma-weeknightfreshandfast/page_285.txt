quick tips

This soup is fine after 20 minutes of simmering, but it’s even better after
45 minutes or reheated on the next night. If the soup gets too thick, thin it
to the desired consistency with more broth or water. This basic recipe is
easily varied: Replace the black beans with white beans or chickpeas;
choose Andouille or kielbasa sausage; or select parsnips, sweet potatoes,
or cabbage in place of the peppers.
