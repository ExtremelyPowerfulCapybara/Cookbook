add a simple side

• Toss butter lettuce with a citrus vinaigrette and top with orange or
  grapefruit segments and chopped fresh chives

• Sauté thinly sliced brussels sprouts in olive oil with red pepper
  flakes until tender, about 6 minutes; sprinkle with lemon juice

• Toss cauliflower florets in olive oil, season with salt and pepper,
  and roast in a 400°F (200°C) oven until tender and browned, 30
  minutes

• Mash roasted sweet potatoes or hard-shelled squash and season
  with brown butter, chopped fresh sage, and grated Parmesan cheese
