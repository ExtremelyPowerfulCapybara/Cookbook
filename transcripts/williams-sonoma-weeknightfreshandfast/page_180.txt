choose fruit for dessert

• Cut firm but ripe pears into slices, then sauté with butter and maple syrup until tender and glazed; spoon over frozen yogurt
• Slice tart apples, preferably local fruit from a nearby farm, and serve with wedges of sharp Cheddar cheese and walnuts
• Cut ripe fresh figs in half, arrange in a baking dish, and bake in a 400°F (200°C) oven until tender and hot, 10–15 minutes; top with Greek yogurt mixed with Port and sugar to taste
