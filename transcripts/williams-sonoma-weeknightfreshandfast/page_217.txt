quick tips

Garam masala, an aromatic Indian spice blend, can be found in the spice section of most supermarkets. It’s a terrific seasoning to have on hand in the cupboard to add flavor to chicken, fish, potatoes, and sweet potatoes. This stew reheats well, so it can be served again a night or two later. Canned chickpeas are a versatile staple; they are great in salads, stews, and soups.
