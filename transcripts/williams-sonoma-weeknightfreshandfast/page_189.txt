spice-crusted tuna with fresh pear
Chutney

Fragrant seeds give the fish a crunchy coating as
well as contribute exotic flavor. Sweet pears mixed
with lime juice, fresh ginger, and spicy chile form a
quick relish. I like to serve this easy dish with
cooked rice and green beans.

Albacore tuna steaks, 4 each about 6 oz (185 g) and 1 inch (2.5 cm) thick
Coriander seeds, 2 teaspoons
Cumin seeds, 2 teaspoons
Lemon zest, 2 teaspoons grated
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Large Bosc pears, 2, firm but ripe, finely diced
Fresh lime juice, 2 tablespoons
Fresh cilantro, 2 tablespoons minced
Fresh ginger, 1 tablespoon peeled and minced
Serano chile, ½ teaspoon seeded and minced
Olive oil, 1 teaspoon

makes 4 servings

• Place the tuna in a shallow baking pan. In a spice mill or mortar with
pestle, coarsely grind the coriander and cumin seeds. Press the seeds into
both sides of the tuna. Sprinkle both sides with the lemon zest, salt, and
pepper. Set aside while making the chutney.
