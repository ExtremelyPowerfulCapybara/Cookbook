• Raise the heat to high and bring the simmering seasoned water to a
boil. Add 1 tablespoon salt and the shrimp. Return the water to a boil.
Reduce the heat and simmer until the shrimp are just cooked through,
about 2 minutes. Drain well.

• Cut the shrimp into ¾-inch (2-cm) pieces and add to the salad. Toss to
combine. Season the salad to taste with salt and pepper, and serve right
away.

quick tips

This recipe doubles easily and can be made 1 day ahead of time. If you
are cooking for two, consider doubling the recipe and serving it again for
an extra-quick dinner the next night. For a sustainable product, look for
wild American shrimp. For even faster preparation, use purchased
cooked shrimp that has already been peeled.
