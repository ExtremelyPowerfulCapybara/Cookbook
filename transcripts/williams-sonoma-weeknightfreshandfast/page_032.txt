Chicken breasts with lentil-raisin-mint
salad

Peppery radishes and refreshing mint deliver bright spring flavors to healthful chicken breast. A quick protein-rich meal.

Brown lentils, 1 cup (7 oz/220 g), picked over and rinsed
Small red onion, 1, cut in half
Bay leaf, 1
Coarse kosher salt
Red wine vinegar, 2 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon
Large celery ribs, 2, finely chopped
Radishes, 1 large bunch, trimmed, quartered lengthwise, and sliced
Fresh mint, 7 tablespoons (⅔ oz/20 g) minced
Extra-virgin olive oil, ½ cup (4 fl oz/125 ml), plus 2 tablespoons
Skinless, boneless chicken breast halves, 4 (about 6 oz/185 g each)
All-purpose flour for dredging

[Separate yield block]
makes 4 servings
