pasta with lamb and rosemary

Ground lamb and zucchini cooked with fresh rosemary and tomatoes creates a succulent—and unexpected—sauce for pasta. Slightly salty pecorino cheese is the perfect garnish.

Olive oil, 2 tablespoons
Large onion, 1, thinly sliced
Fresh rosemary, 1 tablespoon minced
Ground lamb, ½ lb (500 g)
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Dry white wine, ¼ cup (2 fl oz/60 ml)
Diced tomatoes, 2 cans (14.5 oz/435 g each)
Large zucchini, 2 (about 1 lb/500 g total), cut into ½-inch (12-mm) cubes
Rotini or rotelle pasta, preferably multigrain, 10–12 oz (315–375 g)
Romano cheese, ½ cup (2 oz/60 g) grated, plus more as needed

makes 4 servings

• In a large frying pan over medium-high heat, warm the oil. Add the onion and rosemary and sauté until the onion is tender, about 5 minutes. Add the lamb and sprinkle with salt and pepper. Sauté until the lamb is no longer pink, breaking up the meat with a wooden spoon, about 3 minutes. Add the wine and boil until absorbed, about 2 minutes. Add the tomatoes with their juice, and bring to a boil. Mix in the zucchini. Reduce the heat to medium-low and boil slowly until the sauce is thick, stirring occasionally, about 10 minutes.
