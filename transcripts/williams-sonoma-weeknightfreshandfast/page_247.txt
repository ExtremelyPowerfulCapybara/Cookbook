• Drain the pasta and add to the mushroom mixture. Stir over medium

Divide the pasta between 2 warmed shallow bowls. Sprinkle with parsley
and serve right away.

quick tips

Use authentic Parmigiano-Reggiano for the best flavor. This pasta is a
great vehicle for canterelle mushrooms, but more affordable shiitake or
cremini mushrooms are also delicious. For best results, use fettuccine
made with egg; it will be tender and silky after cooking.
