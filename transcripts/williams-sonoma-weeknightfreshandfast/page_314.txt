chicken sauté with thyme, lemon, and
chile

Olive oil, 2 tablespoons
Chicken breast cutlets, (¾ lb/375 g)
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Large shallot, 1, minced
Fresh thyme, 2 teaspoons minced
Serrano chile, ½–1, minced
Dry white wine, ⅓ cup (3 fl oz/80 ml)
Fresh lemon juice, 2 tablespoons

[Separate block: makes 2 servings]

• In a large frying pan over medium-high heat, warm 1 tablespoon of the
oil. Sprinkle the chicken with salt and pepper and add to the frying pan.
Sauté until cooked through, 2–3 minutes per side. Transfer the chicken to
2 warmed plates.

• Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the same frying pan, and then
add the shallot, thyme, and chile. Sauté until the shallot begins to soften,
about 1 minute. Add the wine and lemon juice, and boil, stirring up the
browned bits on the pan bottom, until syrupy, about 1 minute. Remove
