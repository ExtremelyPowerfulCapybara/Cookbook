linguine, shrimp, and endive with
bread crumbs

Shrimp, endive, and lemon, quickly sautéed, make
a novel topping for pasta, but the crisp-herbed-
infused crumbs give it an extra-special touch. The
combination of fresh seafood, vegetables, and grain
are a perfect one-dish meal.

Whole-grain or sourdough bread, 2 slices
Olive oil, 4 tablespoons (2 fl oz/60 ml)
Large shallot, 1, minced
Fresh thyme, 1 tablespoon, plus 2 teaspoons minced
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
Dried linguine, ¾–1 lb (375–500 g)
Large onion, 1, finely chopped
Red pepper flakes, ½ teaspoon
Fresh rosemary, 2 teaspoons minced
Large shrimp, 1 lb (500 g), peeled and deveined
Endive, 4 heads, cut in half lengthwise, and then cut crosswise into ½-inch (12-mm) slices
Fresh lemon juice, 3 tablespoons
Fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
