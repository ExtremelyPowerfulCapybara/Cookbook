roasted mahi mahi with olives, capers,
and lemon

Mahi mahi is an inexpensive, wild ocean fish that is harvested young, so mercury is not a concern. Here, I've topped it with a zesty olive-caper-lemon mixture. Serve it with olive oil-mashed potatoes and wilted greens.

Extra-virgin olive oil, 1 tablespoon, plus more as needed
Mahi mahi fillets, 2 (about 6-7 oz/185-220 g each)
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Pitted kalamata olives, ⅓ cup (2 oz/60 g) chopped
Shallot, 1, minced
Capers, 1½ tablespoons
Fresh lemon juice, 1½ tablespoons
Grated lemon zest, 1 teaspoon

[Separate block: yield]
makes 2 servings

• Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Brush a small glass baking dish with olive oil. Sprinkle the fish fillets on both sides with salt and pepper, place in the dish, and brush with oil on both sides.

• In a small bowl, combine the olives, shallot, capers, lemon juice and zest, and the 1 tablespoon oil; spoon the mixture over the fish. Roast the fish until just cooked through, about 12 minutes.
