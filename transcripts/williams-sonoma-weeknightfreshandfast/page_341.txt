plan ahead for fresh & fast meals

Having on hand a good supply of pantry staples
will provide a solid foundation for putting together
quick meals throughout the week. If your cupboard
is well stocked with such things as dried pastas and
grains, canned beans and tomatoes, a good supply
of oils and vinegars, prepared broths, and flavorful
spices, you should only need to shop a couple times
a week for perishable ingredients, such as fresh
produce, meat, poultry, and fish. Once you
get home, wrap, label, and freeze what you won’t
be using within the next couple of days; remember
to move the frozen items to the refrigerator to thaw
on the night before you will need them.
