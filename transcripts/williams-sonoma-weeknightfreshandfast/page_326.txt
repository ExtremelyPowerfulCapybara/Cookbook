pasta with fennel, tomatoes, olives, and shrimp

This innovative pasta dish starts with a quick
shrimp and fennel stew, which I enhance with
fragrant fennel seeds and shiny black kalamata
olives. Start with a salad of purchased roasted red
peppers, fresh mozzarella, and herbs.

Olive oil, 1½ tablespoons
Fennel seeds, ½ teaspoon
Large fennel bulb, 1, cored and thinly sliced lengthwise, fronds chopped and reserved
Onion, 1, halved and thinly sliced
Red pepper flakes
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
Italian tomatoes, preferably San Marzano, 1 can (14.28 oz/448 g)
Pitted kalamata olives, ¼ cup (1 oz/30 g), quartered lengthwise
Dry white wine, 3 tablespoons
Shrimp, ½ lb (250 g), peeled and deveined
Spaghetti, preferably multigrain, 7–8 oz (220–250 g)

makes 2 servings; can be doubled

• Bring a large pot three-fourths full of salted water to a boil.
• Meanwhile, in a large frying pan over medium-high heat, warm the oil.
