Fall vegetable risotto with blue cheese

This novel take on risotto is packed with
caramelized onions, tender brussels sprouts, earthy
shiitake mushrooms, and creamy blue cheese. It’s a
great way to show off autumn vegetables. Serve an
endive salad to round out the meal.

Low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth, 5 cups (40 fl oz/1.25 l)
Brussels sprouts, 10 oz (315 g), ends trimmed, quartered lengthwise
Olive oil, 2 tablespoons
Large onion, 1, finely chopped
Shiitake mushrooms, ¼ lb (125 g), stems removed, sliced
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Arborio rice, 1½ cups (10½ oz/330 g)
Dry white wine, ½ cup (4 fl oz/125 ml)
Gorgonzola or other blue cheese, ½ cup (3 oz/90 g) crumbled
Walnut pieces, ½ cup (2 oz/60 g), toasted

Separate block: makes 4 servings

• In a saucepan, bring the broth to a boil. Add the brussels sprouts and cook until bright green and almost tender, about 4 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the brussels sprouts to a bowl and set aside. Reduce the heat to low.

• In a heavy medium saucepan over medium-high heat, warm the oil. Add the onion and sauté until golden brown, about 5 minutes. Add the mushrooms, sprinkle with salt and pepper, reduce the heat to medium, and cook, stirring frequently, until they begin to soften, about 3 minutes.
