spaghetti with asian-flavored clams
and zucchini

Toasted sesame oil, ginger, and cilantro lend an
Asian twist to classic pasta with clam sauce.
The zucchini in the sauce makes this a complete
entrée, so there’s no need to plan a side dish. Finish
with fortune cookies and sliced pears.

Spaghetti, preferably multigrain 7–8 oz (200–250 g)
Asian sesame oil, 1 tablespoon
Vegetable oil, 1 tablespoon
Fresh ginger, 1 tablespoon peeled and minced
Large shallot, 1, minced
Small zucchini, 2, cut into ⅓-inch (9-mm) cubes
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Clam juice or low-sodium chicken broth, ½ cup (4 fl oz/125 ml)
Rice vinegar, 1 tablespoon
Asian chile sauce, such as sambal olek or sriracha, 1 teaspoon
Manila clams, 2 lb (1 kg)
Fresh cilantro, ¼ cup (⅓ oz/10 g) minced, plus more for garnish

[Separate block]
makes 2 servings

• Bring a large pot three-fourths full of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta, stir well, and cook until al dente, about 11 minutes. Drain, and then return to the pot. Add the sesame oil and stir to coat. Cover to keep warm.
