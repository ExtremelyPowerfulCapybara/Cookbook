quick tips

This dish is easy, yet elegant enough for a dinner party. You can easily
double or triple it, selecting a roasting pan or two to accommodate all the
fillets in a single layer. For convenience when entertaining, the olive
topping can be made ahead and chilled. Keep olives and capers on hand
to spruce up simple pasta sauces and sautéed vegetables.
