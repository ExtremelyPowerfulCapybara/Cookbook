quick tips

The sauce takes such little time to prepare, it can be made while the pasta
cooks. The mussel mixture would be equally delicious served in shallow
bowls accompanied by crusty bread, rather than over pasta, to soak up
the juices. For an extra special touch, brush the bread with olive oil and
then grill or broil until golden and crunchy on the edges.
