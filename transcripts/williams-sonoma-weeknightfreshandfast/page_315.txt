quick tips

Chicken breast cutlets are a great resource for cooks who have little time
on their hands. I buy organic chicken for the best flavor. The recipe can
be easily doubled, so make a big batch. The next day, cut up the
leftovers, heat the chicken in the remaining sauce, then spoon over hot
cooked rice or noodles.
