Add the rice and stir until opaque, about 1 minute. Add the wine and stir until absorbed. Add about ¾ cup (6 fl oz/180 ml) of the broth, and adjust the heat so the liquid bubbles and is absorbed slowly. Cook, stirring frequently, until the liquid is absorbed. Continue cooking, adding the liquid about ¾ cup at a time and stirring frequently, until the rice is just tender but slightly firm in the center and the mixture is creamy, about 20 minutes. Mix in the brussels sprouts, blue cheese, and a generous amount of pepper. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

• Spoon the risotto into warmed shallow bowls or plates, sprinkle with walnuts, and serve right away.

quick tips

Brief cooking is the key to fresh-tasting brussels sprouts. Here, they are blanched in the same broth that is used to cook the rice, which carries the flavor and nutrients of the brussels sprouts into the dish. Use vegetable broth for a meatless version, or add pancetta to make this more robust.
