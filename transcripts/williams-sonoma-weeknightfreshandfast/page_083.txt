citrus-herb turkey cutlets with baby
broccoli

Tarragon and baby broccoli are abundant in the spring. Here, the ingredients enhance quick-cooking turkey cutlets, which are an economical and healthful base for a meal. Hot cooked rice makes a smart, neutral side dish.

Large orange, 1
Turkey breast cutlets, 1–1¼ lb (500–625 g)
Fresh tarragon, 1 tablespoon, plus 1 teaspoon minced
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Olive oil, 3 tablespoons
All-purpose flour for dredging
Large shallot, 1, minced
Baby broccoli spears, ¾ lb (375 g)
Low-sodium chicken broth, 1 cup (8 fl oz/250 ml)
Unsalted butter, ½ tablespoon (optional)

Separate block:
makes 4 servings

• Finely grate the zest from the orange. Squeeze the juice from the orange and measure ¼ cup (2 fl oz/60 ml) juice. Sprinkle the turkey cutlets on both sides with the orange zest, 1 tablespoon of the tarragon, and salt and pepper.
