• Separate the 6 whole eggs, placing the yolks in a medium bowl and the whites in a large bowl or the bowl of a stand mixer. Add the 2 egg whites to the bowl with the other whites. Gradually whisk about ¼ of the hot milk mixture into the yolks, and then whisk the mixture back into the remaining milk mixture. Mix in the salt, herbs, cheese, and a generous amount of pepper.

• Using an electric mixer, beat the whites until stiff but not dry. Fold ¼ of the whites into the lukewarm cheese mixture to lighten. Fold in the remaining whites. Transfer the mixture to the prepared dish.

• Place the soufflé dish in the oven and reduce the oven heat to 375°F (190°C). Bake until the soufflé is golden, puffed, and gently set in the center, about 35 minutes. Serve right away.

tips

To easily separate the egg yolks from the whites, crack all the eggs into one bowl, and then gently lift out the yolks, allowing the whites to flow through your fingers back into the bowl. The soufflé can be assembled 1 hour ahead of time, covered and left stand at room temperature before baking.
