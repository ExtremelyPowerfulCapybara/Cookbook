quick tips

Turn the leftovers into risotto cakes: For 3–4 cups (15–20 oz/470–630 g) of risotto, mix in 1 egg yolk and ⅓ cup panko (Japanese bread crumbs). Using wet hands, form the mixture into 2-inch (5-cm) balls, then flatten into rounds ¾ inch (2 cm) thick. Dip the cakes into beaten egg and coat with more panko, then brown in olive oil, about 4 minutes per side.
