[Separate block: quick tips]

For a heartier meal with extra protein, add sausage slices, chopped pancetta, a ham hock, or diced smoked turkey. If using the ham hock, simmer it whole in the stew, then remove the meat from the bone, chop it, and stir the chopped meat back into the stew. If you like, rosemary could replace the sage.
