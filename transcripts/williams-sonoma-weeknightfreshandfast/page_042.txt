quick tips

Tilapia is an environmentally sound fish choice; this dish would be
equally delicious with trout, Arctic char, or salmon, which are also
sustainable. The versatile raita can be made a day ahead and chilled. It is
also delicious as a sauce for chicken or as a dressing for potato salad.
