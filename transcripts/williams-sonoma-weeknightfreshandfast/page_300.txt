quick tips

High-quality curry pastes add tons of good flavor to tofu, chicken, or
fish; I always have a few jars in the cupboard. My favorite pastes, made
by Patak, are the spicy Hot Curry, equally spicy Vindaloo, and aromatic
Garam Masala. If you prefer milder food, try the Mild Curry paste. This
dish reheats well, and I always look forward to the even easier dinner on
the next night.
