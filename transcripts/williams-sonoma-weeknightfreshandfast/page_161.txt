quick tips

Because it is so low in fat, bison, also known as buffalo, benefits from
being seasoned well—here, I've used onion, chile, and herbs. It is also
best cooked rare to preserve the natural juices. These patties can be made
with ground beef or turkey and can be seasoned, formed, and refrigerated
1 day ahead of time. If you like, cilantro, thyme, or oregano could replace
the marjoram.
