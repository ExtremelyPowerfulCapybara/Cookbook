quick tips

This recipe yields a generous amount of the piquant sauce, and leftovers can be served with shrimp, fish, or chicken. Although spare ribs generally need to bake for over an hour before grilling, baby back ribs are tender enough to be quickly grilled without precooking—perfect for weeknight meals.
