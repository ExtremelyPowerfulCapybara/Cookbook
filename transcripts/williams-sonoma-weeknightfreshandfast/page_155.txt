quick tips

Boneless chicken thighs take only minutes to grill. If you are cooking for
two, make the entire recipe, then cut up leftover chicken and vegetables
and mix them into the remaining couscous; season the mixture with olive
oil and vinegar for an easy dinner salad the next night. Cut-up cucumber,
tomato, and radishes would be delicious additions. Toasted almonds or
pine nuts would add pleasant crunch.
