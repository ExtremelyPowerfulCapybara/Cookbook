quick tips

Look for ground bison, also called buffalo, at farmers’ markets or natural
food stores. The meat is lower in saturated fat, and higher in omega 3s
than beef, but still has a rich flavor. Ground beef or turkey can replace
the bison. This chili improves with reheating, so save extras for another
meal.
