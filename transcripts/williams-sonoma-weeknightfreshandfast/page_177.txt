Fresh & fast meals for fall

Fall is a transitional season, and one of my favorite
for cooking. The warm days of summer are waning,
but often its bounty has not completely
disappeared. On summers’ heels I look forward to a
plentiful harvest of sweet, golden-fleshed squash;
wild mushrooms; an abundance of roots and tubers;
and sturdy, mature greens such as kale and chard.
Fall is the season that welcomes us back into the
warmth of the kitchen. As you’ll see in the pages
that follow, I like to transform its offerings into
nourishing soups and stews, hearty meat and
poultry braises and roasts, and comforting pasta
dishes.
