Fresh & fast meals for summer

The abundant colors and textures of summer
produce bring new dimension to my cooking. Every
year, I wait eagerly for the truly ripe deep-hued
tomatoes; succulent eggplant and summer squash;
tender sweet corn that will enliven my seasonal
suppers. Summer also marks the season for al
fresco dining and grilling outdoors; often, I find I
just can't face cooking over a hot stove. In this
chapter, you'll discover many a month's worth of
ideas for grilled, simmered, and quickly sautéed
meals, all perfectly suited for the season.
