Arrange the potatoes on the pan in a single layer, leaving a space in the

• Meanwhile, heat the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large frying
pan over medium-high heat. Add the pork and brown on all sides, about 6
minutes.

• Remove the baking sheet from the oven and place the pork in the
center; set the frying pan aside. Brush the pork and potatoes with some of
the molasses mixture. Return the baking sheet to the oven and roast the
potatoes until tender and a thermometer inserted in the thickest part of
the pork registers 145°F (63°C), about 15 minutes. Transfer the pork and
potatoes to a warmed platter.

• Pour off all but 1 tablespoon fat from the frying pan, and then set the
frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the shallot and cook, stirring
frequently and stirring up the browned bits, until it begins to soften,
about 3 minutes. Add the remaining molasses mixture and the broth.
Bring to a boil, stirring frequently. Simmer until the sauce thickens
slightly, about 3 minutes.

• Slice the pork, and then drizzle with the sauce. Serve the pork and
potatoes right away, passing any remaining sauce separately.
