grilled tuna with basil-walnut sauce

This thick sauce shows off summer basil at its best
and is the perfect accent for the gentle flavor of
albacore tuna. Grill olive oil-rubbed bread along
with the fish, and serve briefly sautéed zucchini
ribbons on the side.

Albacore tuna steaks, 2 (about 6 oz/185 g each), 1 inch (2.5 cm) thick
Coarse kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
Grated zest of 1 lemon
Extra-virgin olive oil, 1½ tablespoons, plus more as needed
Fresh basil leaves, ¼ cup (¼ oz/7 g) packed
Walnut pieces, 1 tablespoon

Separate block:
makes 2 servings; can be doubled

• Place the tuna on a plate. Sprinkle both sides with salt, pepper, and the lemon zest. Brush with olive oil. Let marinate while preparing the sauce.

• In the work bowl of a food processor, combine the basil, walnuts, ⅛ teaspoon salt, and a generous amount of pepper. Process until finely ground. With the machine running
