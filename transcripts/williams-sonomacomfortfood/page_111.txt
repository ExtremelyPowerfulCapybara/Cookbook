Sometimes the most basic of ingredients make the most delicious
dishes of all. Of course, to make this dish really shine, seek out
the best quality bread, tomatoes, and olive oil you can find. Serve
as the first course to a summery, grilled meal.

TOMATO BRUSCHETTA

Makes 4-6 servings

Cherry tomatoes, 1 cup (6 oz/190g), halved
Fresh basil, ¼ cup (¼ oz/7 g), sliced
Extra-virgin olive oil, kosher salt, and freshly ground pepper
Pain au levain

In a bowl, mix the tomatoes, basil, and sprinkle generously with oil, salt,
and pepper. Cut the bread into 6 slices, each ½ inch (12 mm) thick.
Heat a heavy grill pan, preferably cast iron, over medium-high heat.
Brush the bread with oil and grill, turning once, until toasted on both
sides, about 2 minutes. Transfer the grilled bread slices to a serving
platter. Mound the tomato salad on top and serve at once.
