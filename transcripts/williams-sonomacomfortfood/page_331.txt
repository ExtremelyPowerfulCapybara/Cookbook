Malted milk powder—just 2 simple tablespoons—transforms a
vanilla milkshake into an irresistible dessert, appreciated by all
old-school palettes. This timeless take on the milkshake imparts
a slightly toasty flavor from the evaporated milk.

VANILLA MALTEDS

Makes 2 servings

Whole milk, ⅓ cup (3 fl oz/80 ml), or as needed
Pure vanilla extract, 1 teaspoon
Malted milk powder, 2 tablespoons
Vanilla bean ice cream, 2½ cups (17½ oz/550 g)
Whipped Cream (see recipe)

In the following order, put the ⅓ cup (3 fl oz/80 ml) milk, vanilla extract,
malted milk powder, and ice cream in a blender. Cover and process until
smooth, adding more milk if necessary to achieve the consistency you
desire.

Pour into 2 tall, chilled glasses, top each with a dollop of whipped cream,
add a tall straw, and serve at once.

Change it up You can easily turn this into a chocolate malt.
Instead of the vanilla bean ice cream, use chocolate ice cream or your
favorite premium-quality chocolate ice cream.
