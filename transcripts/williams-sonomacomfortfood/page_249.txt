Change it up Give the mashed potatoes a boost of flavor with
roasted garlic. Slice a head of garlic in half crosswise, drizzle the halves
with olive oil, wrap them in aluminum foil, and place in a small, shallow
pan. Bake in a preheated 400°F (200°C) oven until the cloves are soft,
about 35 minutes. Squeeze the cloves from their papery sheaths into the
potatoes when you add the milk and whisk to combine.
