Transfer the beef to a plate.

Add another 2 tablespoons of the fat to the pot and heat over medium
heat. Add the onion, carrots, celery and garlic and cook, stirring
occasionally, until the onion softens, about 5 minutes. Stir in the butter
and let it melt. Sprinkle with the flour and stir well. Gradually stir in the
stock, and then stir in the tomato paste, the 1 tablespoon parsley, thyme,
rosemary, and bay leaf. Return the beef to the pot and bring to a boil.
Cover, place in the oven, and cook for 1½ hours.

Cut the unpeeled potatoes into 1-inch (2.5-cm) cubes, add them to the
pot, stir, re-cover, and continue cooking until both the meat and potatoes
are tender, about 45 minutes more. Season the stew with salt and pepper.
Serve at once, garnished with parsley and the reserved bacon.

Lighten it up

If you want to add more vegetables to this dish, sauté
½ pound (250 g) cremini mushrooms, quartered, in 2 tablespoons olive
oil over medium heat until browned, about 5 minutes, and add them to
the stew with the potatoes.
