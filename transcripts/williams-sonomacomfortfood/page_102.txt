Change it up For a devilishly spicy version, omit the herbs and
lemon zest. Stir ½ chipotle chile in adobo, minced, into the mashed egg
yolks. Or go classic and add minced bread-and-butter pickles and 1
teaspoon yellow mustard instead of the herbs and lemon zest.
