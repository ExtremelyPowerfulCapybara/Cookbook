Spread 4 of the bread slices with equal amounts of the egg salad, then top each with a lettuce leaf. Spread mayonnaise on the remaining 4 bread slices, and place, mayonnaise side down, on each sandwich. Cut in half and serve.

Lighten it up
Add chopped fresh herbs, such as tarragon, chervil, or chives, to the egg salad. For easy appetizers, spoon the egg salad into Belgian endive leaves, or for a lunchtime salad, mound it onto a bed of lettuce.
