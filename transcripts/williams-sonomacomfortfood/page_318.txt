Halve the unpeeled bananas lengthwise. Sprinkle the cut sides of each
banana half with 1 teaspoon sugar. Heat a large nonstick frying pan over
medium heat.

In batches to avoid crowding, add the banana halves, cut sides down, and
cook just until the sugar is caramelized, about 30 seconds. Transfer to a
plate.

To make each banana split, peel 2 banana halves and arrange,
caramelized sides up, in a banana-split dish or an oblong bowl. Place 3
scoops of ice cream between the banana halves. Top with the hot fudge
sauce, a sprinkle of the toasted almonds, and a large dollop of whipped
cream. Perch a cherry on top and serve at once.

Change it up
Maybe ice cream parlors make their banana splits with
a scoop each of vanilla, strawberry, and chocolate, and you may wish to
follow suit. Or, use your favorite flavor of ice cream. You can also
substitute toasted peanuts or cashews for the almonds.
