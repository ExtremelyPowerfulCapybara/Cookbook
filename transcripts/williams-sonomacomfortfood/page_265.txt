Cover and refrigerate until chilled, at least 2 hours. Taste and adjust the
seasoning with more vinegar, salt, and pepper before serving. Serve cold.

Change it up If you don't have a food processor, you can ready the
vegetables by hand with a chef's knife and box shredder-grater. And if
you prefer a sweeter coleslaw, stir in a bit of sugar until the flavor suits
you. Thinly sliced red bell pepper and cucumber are also nice additions.
