A handful at a time, squeeze the excess liquid from the spinach, and add
the spinach to the cream cheese mixture. Add the artichoke hearts and
mix well. Spread in the baking dish and sprinkle with the remaining
Parmesan. (The dip can be covered with plastic wrap and refrigerated for
up to 1 day.)

Bake until the dip is bubbling and browned, about 30 minutes. Serve hot,
with the baguette slices for dipping.
