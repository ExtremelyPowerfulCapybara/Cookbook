Change it up
Smoky flavor pairs wonderfully with the natural sweetness of corn. In a frying pan, fry 3 slices of bacon, coarsely chopped, over medium heat until crisp, about 7 minutes, then drain on paper towels. Use the bacon fat in place of the butter, and sprinkle the bacon over the corn just before serving. Or, for a version with smoke and spice, add 1 chipotle chile in adobo sauce, seeded and minced, and 1/2 teaspoon adobo sauce with the cream.
