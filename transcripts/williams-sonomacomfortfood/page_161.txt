Toast the buns. Place the bottom halves of the buns, cut side up, on
warmed individual plates and top with the beef mixture, dividing it
equally. Cover with the bun tops and serve right away.

Lighten it up Sloppy Joes are just as good when made with ground
turkey or ground chicken. Some cooks like to add 1—2 cups (7 oz/220 g
—14 oz/440 g) cooked kidney or pinto beans to the beef mixture just
before it is ready. Or, you can top the beef mixture with thin slices of
Cheddar cheese before covering with the bun tops.
