For many of us, it is unthinkable to eat baked ham without a huge scoop of creamy, cheesy scalloped potatoes alongside. Most old family recipes call for Cheddar cheese and milk, thickened with a sprinkling of flour, but this updated version takes it to a new level, with rich cream, nutty Gruyère, and meltingly tender leeks.

RICH SCALLOPED POTATOES

[Yield]
Makes 8 servings

Unsalted butter, 2 tablespoons, plus more for the baking dish
Leeks, 3 cups (12 oz/375 g) chopped, white and pale green parts
Kosher salt, 2 teaspoons
Freshly ground pepper, ½ teaspoon
Baking potatoes, 3¾ pounds (1.85 kg)
Gruyère cheese, 2 cups (8 oz/250 g) shredded
Heavy cream, 3 cups (24 fl oz/750 ml)

Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Generously butter a 9-by-13-inch (23-by-33-cm) baking dish.

In a large frying pan, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the leeks and cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 7 minutes. Remove from the heat.

Mix together the salt and pepper. Peel the potatoes and thinly slice into rounds.

Spread one-third of the potatoes in an even layer in the prepared baking dish and season with about one-fourth of the salt mixture. Top with one-third of the Gruyère and half of the leeks and season with about one-third of the remaining salt mixture.
