Change it up
For savory biscuits, stir ½ cup (2 oz/60 g) shredded
sharp Cheddar cheese, 1½ tablespoons minced fresh chives, and ¼
teaspoon freshly ground pepper into the flour and butter mixture before
adding the buttermilk. You may need to use a little more buttermilk. The
biscuits won't rise as high, but they will still be delicious.
